## 1. Why the Plan Was Reordered

The next-steps list drafted after session `modelgraph` led with "fine-tune with height variation". Review found two problems with that ordering.

**The tolerance was never measured.** `surface_height_rel_pelvis` carries `tolerance = 0.15 m` in `policy_capability_graph.POLICY_PROFILES` — a hand-authored number. The OOD *verdict* survives any tolerance in [0.05, 0.40] m, but "5.6× tolerance" ranges from 2× to 16× across that interval, and the ranking of `vertical_reach_ood` above `vision_domain_ood` / `arm_laterality_mismatch` is a direct function of the assumed tolerances. Since the ranking selects the remediation and the remediation costs GPU-weeks, it must be measured first.

**Mimic is not turnkey for this.** `G1PickAndPlaceMimicEnvCfg` (`pick_and_place_task.py:391`) hard-raises on anything but `ArmMode.DUAL_ARM` and sets `use_navigation_controller = True` with a four-phase navigate sequence — it is the locomanip config. The static tabletop task has no bespoke G1 mimic cfg. Mimic also retargets segments into new **object frames** (`object_ref` + `selection_strategy="nearest_neighbor_object"`); recorded whole-body posture is replayed, not re-solved. Height augmentation via Mimic is therefore a contingent option whose yield must be falsified, not a cheap first step.

## 2. The Finding That Makes Measurement Cheap

`FIXTURE_SECTOR_BOUNDS["galileo_locomanip"]` (`spatial_geometric_oracle.py:36`) already declares three decks, and `surface_sector` is a first-class spec field consumed by `object_placer.py:532`. **A height sweep is a YAML change — no new assets.**

With the G1 base at world `z = 0` and pelvis at `+0.75`:

| Sector | Deck world z | Rel. pelvis |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| `shelf_tier_1` | −0.03 | **−0.78 m** |
| `shelf_tier_2` | +0.50 | **−0.25 m** |
| `shelf_tier_3` | +0.90 | **+0.15 m** |
| `maple_table` (v9) | ~0.755 | **+0.03 m** |
| `wireshelving_a01` / `tier_1` | +0.76 | **+0.01 m** |

Two properties worth remembering:
- `shelf_tier_1` at −0.78 m lands within 2 cm of the declared corpus invariant (−0.8015 m), independently corroborating it.
- `shelf_tier_3` at +0.15 m is *above* the maple table, so the sweep **brackets** the target rather than extrapolating to it.

The tiers also break the C1 confound: the failing scene varies height, visual domain, laterality, and prompt at once. Holding laterality and prompt at corpus values and sweeping tiers within the galileo visual domain isolates height; `wireshelving` adds a mild visual shift at target height. Five conditions, ~20 episodes each, zero training compute.

## 4. The Constraint That Splits Phase 3

`submodules/Isaac-GR00T/gr00t/configs/finetune_config.py:49-58` — `tune_llm=False`, `tune_visual=False`, `tune_projector=True`, `tune_diffusion_model=True`.

The vision encoder is **frozen by default** (deliberate from N1.5 onward). So:
- **Height** lives in the DiT action head, which *is* tuned — mechanism and lever align.
- **Visual domain** must be absorbed by the projector + DiT learning invariance to a frozen encoder's features. Weaker than "fine-tune the vision tower", and its viability is exactly what the Phase 2 corpus-centroid measurement decides.
