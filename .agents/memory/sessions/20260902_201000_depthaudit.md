## 1. Why Depth Estimation Belongs in the Loop

Teleoperation and real-robot corpora (LeRobot, OpenX, DROID) store **RGB only**. `Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf` recovers dense relative geometry retroactively, so the training distribution's 3D layout can be compared against a candidate scene without re-collecting data — and without running hundreds of rollout steps first.
