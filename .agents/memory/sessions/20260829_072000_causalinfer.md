## 1. Architectural Decisions

1. **Pose resolution as inference, not assignment**:
   - Declarative semantic relations (`on`, `inside`, `nav_corridor`, sector constraints) are compiled into a factor graph over object poses.
   - `relax_spec_spatial_factor_graph()` runs loopy belief propagation to find a mutually consistent placement, rather than resolving relations greedily in declaration order.

2. **Entropy as the convergence signal**:
   - Each reified relation records `prior_entropy` (before relaxation) and `posterior_entropy` (after).
   - A large residual posterior entropy marks an under-constrained relation — the trigger for asking the generator for more constraints.

3. **Intervention vs. observation**:
   - Remediation patches are modeled as *interventions* on the graph (do-operator semantics): re-anchoring a fixture cuts the incoming edges to its pose variable rather than conditioning on it.
   - This keeps `v_{n+1}` derivations interpretable in the PROV-O ledger.

4. **Pre-flight geometric oracle**:
   - `spatial_geometric_oracle.py` validates containment, reachability, and camera-frustum intersection *before* Isaac Sim launches (pure math, no GPU).
