## 1. Empirical Results

| Scenario | N | Settled | Lifted | Placed | Lift→Place Conversion |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Apple → plate (baseline) | 65 | 100% | 86.2% | 12.3% | 14.3% |
| B1 tomato soup → blue bin (v2) | 50 | 100% | 94.0% | 46.0% | 48.9% |
| B1 tomato soup → blue bin (v3) | 52 | 100% | 84.6% | 44.2% | 52.3% |
| B1 tomato soup → blue bin (v4) | 42 | 100% | 88.1% | 14.3% | 16.2% |
| B4 spam can → grey bin | 70 | 100% | 97.1% | 25.7% | 26.5% |

- **Chi-square stage independence** (apple): χ² = 59.51, p = 1.22e-14 — failure is localized to in-flight transport/release, not approach.
- **Geometry dominates grasp**: cylindrical/planar faces (soup can, spam can) lift far more reliably than spherical organics (apple), and convert >3.4x better.
- **v4 regression is the key negative result**: compressing `action_chunk_length` 16 → 8 collapsed conversion from 52.3% to 16.2%. Chunk compression is *not* a monotone improvement; the oracle's rule over-fires.
