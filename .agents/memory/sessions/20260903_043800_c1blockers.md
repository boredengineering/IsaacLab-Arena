## 1. Failure Modes Diagnosed

1. **`v7` — PhysX de-penetration catapult**: the 30 cm `clay_plate` at `X = -0.18 m` overlapped the G1 palm/fingers at `t = 0`; collision de-penetration injected a 1.21 m/s impulse that rocketed the plate across the deck.
2. **`v7` — unpowered joint collapse**: calling `sim.step()` without stepping `action_manager` cuts motor torque on all 29 joints; the upper body collapsed onto the table. **Settling must use `env.step(hold_action)`.**
3. **`v8` — resolved clearance**: table anchored at `X = -0.58 m`, sector bounds pushed to `X_table ∈ [0.48, 0.88]`, 12-step hold-action warmup. Verified drift ≤ 0.07 mm, velocity 0.001 m/s. Phase 1 gate passes — but the policy stood still.
4. **`v9` — false-positive contact termination**: `policy_runner` reported `success_rate: 1.0` from a **0.28 s / 14-frame** episode. The plate rim grazed the apple, `object_on_destination` cleared its 0.1 N force threshold while the apple was at rest, and the env aborted before any grasp. Telemetry disagreed with itself: `success: true` alongside `progress.overall_score: 0.0` with `active_predicates: objects_settled`.
5. **`v9` — scaled-mesh instability**: `scale: [0.5, 0.5, 0.5]` on the plate broke the settle gate (apple 0.79 m/s, 32.1 rad/s). `maple_table_robolab` lacks the invisible flat collision support (`StaticShelfSupport`) and `_USD_ORIGIN_ABOVE_BOTTOM_M` compensation that `galileo_locomanip` provides.

## 2. Why Further Iteration on This Configuration Fails

- `nvidia/GN1x-Tuned-Arena-G1-Static-PickNPlace` is a **task-specific checkpoint fine-tuned on 200 demos inside `galileo_g1_static_pick_and_place`** — not a zero-shot generalist VLA.
- **Vertical OOD**: trained surface sits at ΔZ = −0.8015 m below the pelvis; `maple_table` sits at −0.0126 m. An 80 cm mismatch.
- **Visual OOD**: dark matte shelf + dense packing-box background → bright wood grain in an empty white void under a flat dome light.
- **Prompt/laterality contradiction**: C1 specifies a *right*-arm grasp; 100% of demos are left-arm with the prompt locked to `"move the apple to the plate"`.

## 3. Decision Taken (2026-09-03)

Pathways A (rebuild the benchmark on the shelf scene) and B (full teleop → fine-tune on `maple_table`) were **rejected**. The chosen direction: **fine-tune on the galileo scene and transfer to `maple_table`**, with the pipeline itself responsible for diagnosing and closing the transfer gap. Pathway C (sequential termination guard) is implemented regardless — it is a correctness fix for all manipulation tasks, not a fix for C1.

Follow-on work is tracked in session `modelgraph`.
