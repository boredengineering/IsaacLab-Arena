## 1. Source-backed findings

### R1 — Canonical identity is a migration boundary

[contracts.py:389–455](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/contracts.py#L389) hashes the model's complete serialized form. ### R2 — Parameterizing the evaluator alone leaves a hidden native veto

[scene_observation.py:163–172](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/scene_observation.py#L163) uses fixed thresholds and one criterion subject; [native_capture.py:64–79](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/native_capture.py#L64) pins several values. More importantly, [native_realization.py:18–30,194–260](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/native_realization.py#L18) has `LINEAR_SPEED_LIMIT`, no linear-limit field in `NativeSettleSettings`, and raises `SceneSettleRejected`. [NativeCaptureProducer](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/native_capture.py#L668) wraps that as a failed capture before normal assessment. New parameters must reach mechanics, sampling, retained reports and evaluation. A complete finite measurement that violates a chosen criterion must not automatically become an integration failure. Keep reset/termination, invalid data, unsafe execution and retention failures distinct.

### R3 — The existing ternary codec is not a general temporal/aggregation codec

[visual_request and validate_visibility_response](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/scene_observation.py#L229) require a dense window, bounded frames, one ordered camera occurrence for `visibility-v2`, exact answer ordering and a hardwired ALL-style fold. The response does not explicitly identify step/modality, and its limitations say retained imagery. The full-scene implementation needs versioned frame identities and declared camera/subject/time aggregation; simply calling the I02 parser is insufficient. Preserve the old codec's meaning. Core evidence already has inconclusive verdicts—do not build a parallel truth framework.

### R4 — Differing windows affect requests, restoration and displacement, not only DTOs

[scene_ports.py:393–486](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/scene_ports.py#L393) selects a displacement sample from the capture-window start and restores exactly one measured manifest plus at most one visual manifest. New sparse/multi-stream coverage must reach all these consumers. Retain coherent candidate/reset/clock identities and deterministic selected-evidence ancestry; never fuse unrelated cohorts to satisfy a conjunction.

### R5 — Retained threshold exploration is a new derivation, not ordinary replay

### R12 — False visibility does not supply a causal diagnosis or repair target

The current [router](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/scene_loop.py#L341) chooses from aggregate evidence, while [repair validation](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/repairs.py#L275) constrains an authored delta. Retain the failed subject/criterion/frame and a supported mechanism; require appropriate prerequisites and a permissible effective intervention. Preserve complete uncertainty and sensor conflict. ### R13 — The oracle blueprint exceeded the available instrumentation

[make_native_sampler](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/scene_observation.py#L465) samples world poses/velocities and narrowly filtered contact-force tensors, not contact manifolds, center-of-mass support polygons or general occlusion. Camera/depth/instance metadata and actual support mapping require scoped implementation and empirical validation. Remove deterministic-glass-box, exact occlusion-percentage, universal support, 2 ms counterfactual and general reachability claims. A coarse geometric screen may reject a proposal only under its documented assumptions; it never substitutes for the native witness.

### R14 — Repairable generation needs both semantic and byte-level binding

[BoundedSceneModels._proposal](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/scene_engines.py#L140) validates `ArenaEnvGraphSpec`, not the chosen repair grammar/capabilities. Preserve raw source bytes, canonical candidate bytes and validated semantic identity explicitly; do not rewrite an existing candidate merely to pass full-scene validation. ### R15 — Recovery is stage reconciliation, not a resumable physics checkpoint

[restore_observations](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/scene_ports.py#L439) and [application.resume](../../../../isaaclab_arena/agentic_environment_generation/workflow/application.py#L972) recover selected retained results and eligible unreleased continuations. The native worker does not serialize a complete resumable simulator state. After a released process crashes, a new simulator from the same seed is a new realization/effect, not continuation at the last logged step. Test lost admission ACK, committed-release/unknown-dispatch, retained-result/before-adoption, cancellation/completion races, expiry and cleanup/retention failure at the actual existing boundaries. Preserve `DiagnosticRetentionFailed` as a non-retryable retention failure and clean up even when diagnostic retention fails. Never recover by clearing metadata, dirty flags or locks.

Two native releases can establish at most one baseline/intervention pair in the minimal G3 procedure. They cannot guarantee separate calibration, alternative acquisition, matched controls, repeatability and fault-injection witnesses. G2 must produce an explicit witness-to-case matrix and G3 must execute only its issued cases. ## 4. Shared actor–critic protocol (AC-I03)

<a id="ac-i03"></a>

Every proposed goal incorporates this protocol. The parent is the ACTOR, sole writer/operator and final acceptance owner. A critic's verdict is advice, not execution authority.

1. Use one fresh independent READ-ONLY CRITIC at a time via `delegate_task(tasks=[{goal, context, output_schema}])`. Provide exact acceptance criteria, scoped source/diff, public evidence, authority/effect boundaries and actual remaining case permissions. Do not delegate parallel implementation or create another agent framework.
2. Before a meaningful boundary, state the concrete obligation/hypothesis, smallest correction/check and predicted observation. Critics may inspect public source/evidence only; no edits, tests, constructors, services, application requests, private files/credentials, provider/native calls or database writes.
3. Require `CONTINUE`, `ACCEPT`, `BLOCKED` or `NEED_EVIDENCE`, with criterion, source/evidence anchor, material issue and smallest discriminating next action/effects. `CONTINUE` is not proof or permission. Request one focused clarification of an unusable critique rather than opening a general audit.
4. Parent verifies findings and makes supported in-scope corrections. Normal checkpoints are a pre-boundary critique and a final outcome critique, plus a focused fresh delta critique when material semantics change—not a review between every worker or routine edit. No extra operator approval for routine work already within an issued scope.
5. Stop after three unsuccessful corrections to the same defect. Preserve counts across contexts/reissues. Honor [Plan 04's investigation ceilings](../event_mapping/event-mapping-refactoring_plan_04.md#8-anti-dopamine-invariants--investigation-budgets) for a new architectural blocker: two hypothesis/challenge rounds, three isolated diagnostic invocations and 60 minutes of active investigation. These agent-debugging bounds are not simulation runtime/deadline gates.
6. Complete needed containment/cleanup before waiting on a final critic. Parent closes only witnessed criteria. Report the furthest installed boundary and the exact remaining decision; test totals, inspection packets, plans and review passes do not substitute for application acceptance.
